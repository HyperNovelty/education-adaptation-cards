# Code of Conduct

This project is a small public workspace for synthetic education adaptation card
fixtures, local validators, and review-only rendered dossiers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific in issues, reviews, and pull requests.
- Keep claims modest and tied to the fixture or code being discussed.
- Use synthetic or public-safe examples only.
- Respect the project boundary: no student data, no grades, no LMS/account
  actions, no public distribution claims, and no compliance claims.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety wording, and repository hygiene.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Posting student data, credentials, private records, or real account material.
- Representing these fixtures as classroom-approved, compliance-ready, graded, or
  adopted by any institution.
- Pressuring contributors to publish, deploy, integrate accounts, collect learner
  data, or make external education claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions that violate this
conduct note or the repository safety policy. Repeated or serious violations may
lead to blocked participation.
